Aadu Huli — goats hold the line, tigers hunt the gaps.

A board-and-capture game played across village courtyards in India and Nepal for centuries, known regionally as Aadu Puli Aattam, Bagh-Chal or Meen Mahasangram — long before dice and screens, children and elders played this on stone floors with tamarind seeds and pebbles.
